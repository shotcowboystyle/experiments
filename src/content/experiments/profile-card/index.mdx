Thick borders, an offset shadow with no blur, and a hazard-stripe header. Brutalism is mostly about refusing to soften anything.

## How it works

The hard shadow is a `box-shadow` with zero blur, offset 8px down and to the right. The header stripes are a `repeating-linear-gradient` laid over flat yellow, and the oversized initials behind the avatar are the same letters in 8% black.
