Two portfolio layouts wired with canvas particles. Cards power on as they scroll into view, headlines scatter in letter by letter, and the contact links build a charge when you hover or focus them.

## How it works

Each card and link gets its own particle canvas, animated with GSAP. A small conductor hands out the loud moments, a discharge or a headline glitch, one at a time with a rest between them, so the grid never fires everywhere at once.

The portrait runs through an SVG filter that posterizes it to four flat inks. Pause motion stops every loop. With reduced motion, the grids render still and the button is gone.
